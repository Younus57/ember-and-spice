Our Story

A TABLE BUILT ON FIRE, FLAVOUR & HERITAGE

Ember & Spice is a fictional premium dining room shaped by smoke, memory and craft. Our kitchen draws from India's regional larders—Kashmiri morels, coastal pepper, saffron, stone-ground masalas—and reframes them with contemporary restraint.

Every plate is composed for contrast: live-fire depth, bright chutneys, hand-finished breads and modern presentations that keep tradition at the centre.

Chef Armaan Rao
